Who are we?
By Developer, For Developer, With Developer

Huhu.io is a global community of coders dedicated to making cool stuff coders need and want. We focus on enabling the developer community by curating, incubating, and launching tools based on great ideas, providing support and funding that allows our engineers to develop what they want, the way they want.

With plenty of experience across various areas of expertise—Rust, Go, or Java, for example—we’ve developed a bunch of open-source search extensions and productivity tools we use to make our jobs easier and more rewarding. We believe that—by providing original, well-designed tools with a great UI/UX—we can make a small contribution to the big world of tech. And we want to share them with you!

We’re also smart enough to know we don’t have a monopoly on great ideas. There’s a bunch of really smart folk out there, and—if you’re reading this—you’re probably one of them. So if you have any great ideas for new tools—or how we can improve the ones we already have—please don’t hesitate to let us know. We’re all ears!

Just leave a message on our contact page, and we’ll be in touch.
